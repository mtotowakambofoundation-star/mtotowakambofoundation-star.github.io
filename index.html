About Us

Working together for stronger communities.

Mtoto Wakambo Foundation is a community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

Our approach is based on collaboration, compassion, accountability and practical solutions that respond to the needs of communities.

  • Supporting children and families
  • Strengthening communities
  • Promoting education and opportunity
  • Supporting sustainable development

Our Direction

Mission & Vision

Our work is guided by a clear commitment to people, opportunity and sustainable community development.

Our Mission

To create opportunities and support sustainable community development by empowering children, families and communities through practical, inclusive and people-centered initiatives.

Our Vision

A Tanzania where children, families and communities have the opportunity, resources and support they need to thrive and build a better future.

Our Programs

Areas Where We Create Impact

Our programs are designed around practical community needs and long-term positive change.

01 — CHILDREN

Children & Family Support

Supporting vulnerable children and families through initiatives that promote dignity, opportunity and wellbeing.

02 — EDUCATION

Education & Skills

Supporting access to learning, skills development and opportunities that help people reach their potential.

03 — COMMUNITY

Community Development

Working with communities to identify challenges and develop practical solutions that create lasting benefits.

04 — LIVELIHOODS

Economic Opportunities

Supporting initiatives that encourage entrepreneurship, skills and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Building relationships with individuals, organizations and partners who share our commitment to positive change.
