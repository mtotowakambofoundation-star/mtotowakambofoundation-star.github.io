Who We Are

About Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation

A community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

Building Hope. Changing Lives.

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to creating opportunities, supporting vulnerable communities and strengthening sustainable development in Tanzania.

We believe that every child, family and community deserves an opportunity to build a better future.

Through partnerships, volunteers, donors and community participation, we work to create meaningful and lasting change.
