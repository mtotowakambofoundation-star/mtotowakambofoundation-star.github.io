Who We Are

About Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation

A community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development.

Building Hope. Changing Lives.

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is committed to supporting children, vulnerable individuals and communities through meaningful programs and partnerships.

Our work focuses on creating opportunities, strengthening communities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

  • Community development
  • Support for vulnerable communities
  • Education and opportunities
  • Partnerships and volunteering
  • Sustainable development
