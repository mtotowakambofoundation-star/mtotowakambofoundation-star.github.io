Who We Are

About Mtoto Wakambo Foundation

A community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development.

Building Hope. Changing Lives.

Mtoto Wakambo Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

We believe that every child, family and community deserves an opportunity to grow, learn, participate and build a better future.

Through partnerships, volunteers, donors and community members, we work to develop practical initiatives that create lasting positive change.

Our Commitment

To create opportunities and support sustainable development while empowering communities and improving the lives of children and families.

What We Do

Our Programs

Our work focuses on practical programs that respond to community needs and create sustainable opportunities.

Education & Children

Supporting children through education, learning opportunities, mentorship and initiatives that help them reach their potential.

Community Development

Supporting community-led initiatives that improve livelihoods, participation and sustainable development.

Volunteer & Partnerships

Connecting volunteers, organizations, donors and partners who want to contribute to positive change.

Family Support

Supporting vulnerable families and creating opportunities that contribute to stronger and healthier communities.

Youth Empowerment

Encouraging young people to develop skills, confidence, entrepreneurship and leadership capabilities.

Sustainable Development

Supporting initiatives that promote long-term community resilience and sustainable development.
