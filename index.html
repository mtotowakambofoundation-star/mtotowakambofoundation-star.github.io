About Moto Wakambo Foundation

Moto Wakambo Foundation is committed to supporting communities and individuals through practical programs that create hope, opportunity and positive change.

Our work focuses on strengthening communities, supporting vulnerable people and promoting sustainable development through partnerships and community participation.
