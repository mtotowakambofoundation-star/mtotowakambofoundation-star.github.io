Who We Are

About Mtoto Wakambo Foundation

A community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

Building Hope. Changing Lives.

Mtoto Wakambo Foundation is committed to creating opportunities for children, families and communities.

We believe that every child and every community deserves an opportunity to learn, grow, participate and build a better future.

Through partnerships, volunteers, donors and community members, we support practical initiatives designed to create positive and sustainable change.

Our Commitment

To create opportunities, empower communities and support sustainable development in Tanzania.
