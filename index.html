About Us

Creating Opportunities for a Better Future

Moto Wakambo Foundation is a community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

We believe that every child, family and community deserves an opportunity to grow, learn and build a better future.

Through education, community development, child support and empowerment initiatives, we work together with communities and partners to create meaningful and lasting change.
