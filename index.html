WHO WE ARE

About Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation

Creating hope, opportunities and stronger communities across Tanzania.

Building Hope. Changing Lives.

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to creating opportunities, supporting vulnerable communities and contributing to positive and sustainable development in Tanzania.

We believe that every child, family and community deserves an opportunity to grow, participate and build a better future.

  • Supporting vulnerable children and families
  • Strengthening communities
  • Creating sustainable opportunities
  • Promoting education and empowerment
  • Supporting positive community development
WHAT WE DO

Our Programs

Our programs focus on practical solutions that create lasting positive change.

Supporting vulnerable children by promoting protection, care, dignity and access to opportunities that improve their wellbeing.

🎓

Education

Supporting educational opportunities and encouraging children and young people to develop the knowledge and skills they need for a better future.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

To create opportunities, support vulnerable children and communities, strengthen local capacity and promote sustainable development through partnerships, innovation and community participation.
