About Us

Creating Opportunities. Changing Lives.

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

We work with communities, children, young people, families, partners, volunteers and other stakeholders to build stronger and more resilient communities.

  • Supporting vulnerable children and families
  • Promoting education and opportunities
  • Empowering young people and communities
  • Supporting sustainable community development
What We Do

Our Programs

Our programs are designed to create practical opportunities and improve the lives of vulnerable people and communities.

Education

Supporting access to education, learning opportunities, school materials and skills development for children and youth.

Community Empowerment

Empowering communities through opportunities, skills, partnerships and sustainable development initiatives.

Child & Family Support

Supporting vulnerable children and families and connecting them with opportunities that can improve their future.
