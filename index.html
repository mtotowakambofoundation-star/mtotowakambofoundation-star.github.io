ABOUT US

Building Hope. Changing Lives.

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to creating opportunities, supporting vulnerable communities and contributing to positive and sustainable development in Tanzania.

We believe every child and every vulnerable member of society deserves dignity, opportunity, protection and a chance to build a better future.

Through community initiatives, partnerships, volunteers and responsible support, we work together with communities to create meaningful and lasting change.

Children & Families Supporting children and families facing vulnerability.
Community Development Strengthening communities through sustainable initiatives.
Partnerships Working with partners, volunteers and supporters to create positive change.
WHAT WE DO

Our Programs

Our programs are designed to respond to community needs and create sustainable opportunities for children, families and vulnerable communities.

Education & Children

Supporting access to education, learning opportunities, essential school needs and a brighter future for children.

Community Support

Supporting vulnerable families and communities through practical community-based initiatives.

Youth Empowerment

Creating opportunities that help young people develop skills, confidence and pathways toward a better future.

Vision & Mission
