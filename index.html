ABOUT US

Moto Wakambo Foundation

Moto Wakambo Foundation is a community-focused foundation dedicated to creating opportunities and supporting people and communities to achieve sustainable positive change.

We believe that strong communities are built through education, opportunity, partnerships, empowerment and responsible community development.

Supporting education, skills development and opportunities that help children and young people build a better future.
