Who We Are

About Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation

A community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting sustainable development in Tanzania.

Building Hope. Changing Lives.

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to supporting children, vulnerable communities and sustainable development.

We believe that every child, family and community deserves opportunities to build a better and more dignified future.

  • Children and youth support
  • Education and skills opportunities
  • Community development
  • Volunteer partnerships
  • Sustainable development
