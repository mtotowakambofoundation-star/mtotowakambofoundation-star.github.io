Our Mission

To create opportunities and improve the well-being of children and vulnerable communities through education, health, empowerment and sustainable initiatives.

◉

Our Vision

A society where every child and community member has access to opportunities that enable them to live a dignified and fulfilled life.

♡

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Accountability, Respect, Teamwork and a commitment to making a lasting positive impact.

Building a Better Future Together

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is committed to creating opportunities and supporting vulnerable children, families and communities across Tanzania.

Through education, health, economic empowerment and community development, we work with communities and partners to create sustainable solutions.

We believe that every child deserves hope, opportunity, protection and a chance to build a better future.

Our Programs

We focus on key areas that create real and lasting change.

Education & Learning

Supporting access to quality education, school supplies and learning environments for a brighter future.

Health & Wellbeing

Improving access to health services, nutrition and hygiene awareness for healthier communities.

Empowerment

Equipping individuals with skills, resources and confidence to build sustainable livelihoods.

Community Development

Investing in community projects that improve infrastructure, clean water and local development.
