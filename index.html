Mtoto Wakambo Foundation

Mtoto Wakambo Foundation is a community-focused foundation committed to supporting children, families and communities in Tanzania.

We believe every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow, develop their potential and live in a safe and supportive environment.

Through partnerships, community initiatives and responsible resource mobilization, we work to create lasting positive change.

Our Commitment

We are committed to building stronger communities by supporting education, child wellbeing, community development, empowerment and sustainable opportunities.


Our work is guided by compassion, integrity, accountability, inclusion and measurable impact.
