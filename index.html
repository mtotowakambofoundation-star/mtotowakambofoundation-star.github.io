Who We Are

Mtoto wa Kambo Foundation is a growing community-focused foundation committed to creating opportunities and supporting vulnerable children, families and communities.

Our Commitment

We believe that every child deserves hope, dignity, opportunity and a supportive environment in which to grow.

As a young foundation, we are starting with practical, community-based initiatives and building our impact step by step.

Our approach is simple: listen to communities, identify real needs and work together to create sustainable solutions.
