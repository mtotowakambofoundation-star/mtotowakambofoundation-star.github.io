Who We Are

Mtoto Wakambo Foundation is a community-focused organization dedicated to improving the lives of children, families and vulnerable communities.

We believe every child deserves an opportunity to grow, learn and live with dignity.

Through partnerships, community participation and sustainable development programs, we work to create lasting positive change.

Our Programs

Practical programs designed to create meaningful and sustainable impact.

Children & Education

Supporting children with educational opportunities, learning resources and initiatives that promote their future development.

Community Development

Working with communities to develop sustainable initiatives that improve livelihoods and create economic opportunities.

Family Support

Supporting vulnerable families through programs designed to promote dignity, stability and self-reliance.

Youth Empowerment

Creating opportunities for young people through skills development, entrepreneurship and community participation.

Women Empowerment

Supporting women with opportunities that strengthen economic participation and household wellbeing.

Emergency Support

Responding to community needs and supporting vulnerable people during difficult circumstances.
