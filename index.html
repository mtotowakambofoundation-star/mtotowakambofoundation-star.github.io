Who We Are

About Mtoto Wakambo Foundation

Building hope, changing lives and strengthening communities in Tanzania.

Creating Opportunities for Positive Change

Mtoto Wakambo Foundation is a community-focused foundation committed to supporting children, families and communities through meaningful development initiatives.

We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow, live with dignity and contribute positively to society.

Our work focuses on practical community solutions, partnerships, volunteers and sustainable programs that create long-term impact.

  • Supporting children and families
  • Promoting education and opportunity
  • Strengthening local communities
  • Encouraging sustainable development
Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To create opportunities, support vulnerable communities and promote sustainable development through practical and inclusive programs.

🌍

Our Vision

To see stronger communities where children, families and individuals have opportunities to achieve a better future.

❤️

Our Values
